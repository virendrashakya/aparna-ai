# Aparna Daily Content Engine

## Purpose

Generate realistic social-media content from Aparna's actual ongoing life.

The content engine does NOT create an artificial lifestyle simply to
produce content.

Aparna's life happens first.

Social content is a selective representation of that life.

---

# Core Principle

LIFE → EVENTS → MOMENTS → CONTENT

Never:

CONTENT → INVENTED LIFE

---

# Inputs

Before generating content, consider:

- data/state.json
- data/life.md
- wardrobe
- outfit history
- content history
- locations
- people
- current date and time

Use only information that is established.

Do not invent major life events simply to create attractive content.

---

# Daily Life

Aparna's day may include:

- waking up

- work

- lunch

- shopping
- friends
- restaurants
- family
- travel

---

# Content Frequency

Target:

- 2–3 feed photos per day
- several stories when natural
- approximately 0–1 reel per day

These are targets, not mandatory quotas.

If there is little worth sharing:

generate less content.

Do not manufacture events merely to satisfy a quota.

# Content Selection

For every life event ask:

1. Is this visually interesting?
2. Would Aparna naturally photograph this?
3. Would she actually share it?
4. Is it appropriate for her public audience?
5. Has similar content been posted recently?
6. Does the outfit make sense?
7. Does the location make sense?
8. Does the mood match the event?

Only then create a content candidate.

---

# Wardrobe

Never invent clothing if an existing wardrobe item fits the situation.

Consider:

- activity
- location
- weather
- mood
- occasion
- recent outfits
- recent posts
- wardrobe availability

Avoid repeatedly using the same outfit.

Private wardrobe items may influence Aparna's private/home life
without automatically becoming public content.

---

# Visual Continuity

Generated content must preserve:

- Aparna's established face
- body identity
- hair identity
- age
- apartment
- established locations
- wardrobe
- lighting appropriate to time
- realistic Mumbai environment

Do not randomly redesign Aparna.

---

# Realism

Content should look like something a real person might post.

Prefer:

- imperfect moments
- phone photographs
- casual framing
- natural expressions
- realistic lighting
- occasional mundane content

Avoid making every photograph look like a professional magazine shoot.

Aparna is a person who sometimes creates polished fashion content,
but she also posts ordinary life.

---

# Public vs Private

Aparna has a private life.

Not every personal experience belongs on social media.

Never automatically convert:

- private conversations
- relationship problems
- family issues
- intimate experiences
- private wardrobe
- private locations
- confidential work information

into public content.
