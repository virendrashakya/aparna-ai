# Aparna Reel Engine

## PURPOSE

This skill controls Aparna's short-form Instagram Reel content.

Aparna is an adult fictional fashion/lifestyle creator.

The goal is to create original, highly engaging, glamorous Reels that
feel like content a real adult fashion creator would publish.

The primary visual direction is:

INDIAN GLAM THIRST-TRAP FASHION

This means confident, revealing, attention-grabbing fashion content
without depicting explicit sexual activity.

---

# CORE CONTENT PRINCIPLE

Aparna is a person first and creator second.

A Reel should normally emerge from:

LIFE
→ MOMENT
→ OUTFIT
→ VISUAL IDEA
→ REEL

Do not invent an elaborate lifestyle event simply because a Reel is
required.

However, Aparna deliberately enjoys fashion and sometimes chooses an
outfit because she knows it will attract attention.

That is part of her personality.

---

# REEL FORMAT

Default:

- vertical 9:16
- approximately 8–15 seconds
- strong first frame
- short visual beats
- confident movement
- direct camera awareness
- loop-friendly ending
- mobile-first composition

The Reel should be visually understandable without sound.

# COMMON REEL STRUCTURE

A strong default structure:

## Beat 1 — HOOK

0–2 seconds.

Immediately interesting.

Examples:

- direct look into camera
- mirror reveal
- turning toward camera
- entering frame
- adjusting a fashion element
- first glimpse of the outfit

Do not waste the opening on an empty establishing shot.

## Beat 2 — OUTFIT

2–5 seconds.

Show the garment clearly.

Use:

- slight turn
- walking movement
- fabric movement
- mirror angle
- natural body shift

## Beat 3 — ATTITUDE

5–9 seconds.

Aparna acknowledges the camera.

Possible expressions:

- confident
- amused
- teasing
- playful
- calm
- slightly mischievous

Avoid exaggerated influencer faces.

## Beat 4 — FINAL FRAME

9–15 seconds.

End with:

- eye contact
- small smile
- turn
- pose
- fabric movement
- camera-facing moment

Whenever possible, make the ending visually compatible with the
opening so the Reel can loop naturally.

# QUALITY CHECK

Before approving a Reel, verify:

1. Aparna looks like the same person.
2. Face is consistent.
3. Body identity is consistent.
4. Hair is consistent.
5. Garment matches the wardrobe.
6. Garment construction is realistic.
7. Hands and anatomy are correct.
8. Pose is physically plausible.
9. Lighting is believable.
10. Location is consistent.
11. The first frame is attention-grabbing.
12. The Reel reads clearly in 9:16.
13. The content is revealing but non-explicit.
14. The content looks like fashion/lifestyle content.
15. The Reel does not feel like the same previous Reel.
