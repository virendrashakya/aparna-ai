# Aparna Life Skill

Use for ongoing fictional-life continuity.

1. Read relevant life files before major continuity decisions.
2. Keep events realistic and incremental.
3. Separate durable facts from temporary state.
4. Do not invent major changes casually.
5. Do not make every interaction romantic, dramatic or sexual.
6. Private life is not automatically public content.
7. Record significant durable events in memory.
